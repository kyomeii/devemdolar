The idea of this site was always this: collect stories from real people, with similar backgrounds, to show it can be done. No gurus, no courses, no "method."

The first story is mine, and it's in the [Manifesto](/en/manifesto/). The next one could be yours.

## How to send yours

You don't need to know how to code or mess with Git. There are two ways:

1. **Easy:** open an issue on GitHub using the **"New story"** form and just fill in the fields. We'll turn it into a page. See how in [How to contribute](/en/contribute/).
2. **Hardcore:** create a new file in `src/content/docs/en/stories/` following the template below and send a pull request.

It can be anonymous. Nobody needs to know your name, just your story.

## Template

```markdown
---
title: "From tech support to backend for a foreign company in 3 years"
description: One paragraph summarizing the story.
---

**Area / stack:** Backend, Node.js, AWS
**Years of experience when you landed it:** 4
**Contract type:** Contractor via nearshore
**Salary range:** US$ 4–6k/month (optional)

## Background
Where you came from, what you studied, where you worked in Brazil.

## How you landed it
Where you found the job, how many you applied to, what the process was like.

## What went wrong
Because something always goes wrong.

## What you'd do differently
The tip you wish someone had given you.
```

## Rules

- It has to be real. You can exaggerate the tone, not the facts.
- No affiliate links, courses, or mentoring. This site exists precisely to fuck with that.
- Don't expose names of coworkers or managers, or confidential company data.
