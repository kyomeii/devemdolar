## Talent platforms (nearshore)

You go through a hiring process with the platform, and they introduce you to foreign companies. They usually pay less than direct hiring, but it's the easiest way in.

| Platform | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Revelo](https://www.revelo.com) | Brazilian, connects Latin American devs with American companies |
| [Strider](https://www.onstrider.com) | Brazilian, same idea, focused on devs |
| [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com) | "Top 3%" freelancer network, heavy screening process |
| [Turing](https://www.turing.com) | Remote dev platform, lots of AI/model-training jobs |
| [Arc.dev](https://arc.dev) | Remote jobs and freelance gigs for devs |
| [Andela](https://www.andela.com) | Started in Africa, now hires in Latin America too |
| [Lemon.io](https://lemon.io) | Freelance gigs for startups |
| [BairesDev](https://www.bairesdev.com) | Giant Argentine nearshore, hires a lot of Brazilians |

## Remote job boards

| Site | Notes |
|---|---|
| [HiringCafe](https://hiringcafe.com) | Aggregates straight from the ATSs. The best one, in my opinion |
| [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com) | Formerly AngelList, startup jobs |
| [We Work Remotely](https://weworkremotely.com) | One of the oldest remote job boards |
| [Remote OK](https://remoteok.com) | Lots of jobs, filter by "worldwide" |
| [Remotive](https://remotive.com) | Curated, less garbage |
| [Himalayas](https://himalayas.app) | You can filter by which countries the company accepts |
| [Working Nomads](https://www.workingnomads.com) | Remote job aggregator |
| [Get on Board](https://www.getonbrd.com) | Focused on Latin America |
| [Remotar](https://remotar.com.br) | Brazilian, remote jobs (not all of them for foreign companies) |

## Hiring and payment platforms

These aren't places to find jobs, but you'll run into them once you're hired. See more in [On the job](/en/on-the-job/).

| Platform | What for |
|---|---|
| [Deel](https://www.deel.com) | The company hires you and pays you through it |
| [Remote](https://remote.com) | Same thing |
| [Oyster](https://www.oysterhr.com) | Same thing |
