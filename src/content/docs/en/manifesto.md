I'm building this collaborative site to help other developers escape the *hellhole* of getting paid in shitcoin (the Brazilian real) and actually win their financial freedom.

This content is aimed at professionals who **already have experience in tech**. If you don't, I'm sorry, but $20 of Claude is worth more than your salary.

## Who I am

For background, I've been working for foreign companies since 2024. Here's my career path (you don't have to believe me, and I'm not posting more detailed personal info because I'd rather not get doxxed):

1. I studied mechanical engineering (still haven't graduated) and landed an internship at a Brazilian consulting firm focused mostly on data analysis.
2. Got hired full-time, stayed a while longer, then moved to another consulting firm.
3. After a while at that second firm, I landed my first contract with an American startup (**5k USD/month**), and kept doing both jobs.
4. A few months later, I got my second foreign contract, this time with a consulting firm (**another 5k USD/month**). I quit my CLT job (Brazil's formal employment regime) and focused on the two foreign contracts.
5. And that's how I stayed, making a total of **10k USD/month** for over a year, until I got canned from both, practically at the same time.
6. I was unemployed for a few months, then landed another **5k USD/month** contract at another startup, which is my best job so far.
7. Not long after, I got another contract, a bit smaller this time (**3k USD/month**).

I'm sure there are plenty of people way more qualified than me to talk about this, and that's exactly why this platform is open for everyone to share.

## Why this site exists

My idea with this site is to collect stories from other people with a background similar to mine to help others, and to **fuck over the course-sellers and "mentors"** who charge an absurd amount for content you can find for free on the internet.

If you're still lazy enough to think those services are worth it, don't even bother paying, because you'll always be a mediocre professional, and you deserve to keep making 6k reais as an in-office senior in São Paulo.

I'm going to cover the main topics you need to master to land the job, from the most basic requirement all the way to how to behave once you're in:

1. **English** — without it, don't even start.
2. **Technical** — actually master your stack.
3. **Resume** — beat the ATS and sell yourself.
4. **LinkedIn** — profile, connections, and playing the algorithm's little game.
5. **Job hunting** — where the good jobs are and how to find them before everyone else.
6. **Interview** — the peak of it all.
7. **On the job** — you got it? Now don't fuck it up.

## Open source

I'm not trying to make anything off of this, so I'm leaving the repository **open source** so other people can share more tips and experiences. Found something wrong, outdated, or got a story to tell? Send a pull request.

But I sell out real easy, so if this thing blows up, yes, feel free to send your offers for ads or whatever.
